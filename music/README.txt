SPACE CONTROL - YOUR MUSIC
==========================

Put your own songs in this folder and the game plays them instead of its
built-in soundtrack.

  - Formats: MP3, OGG (Vorbis) and WAV.
  - Songs play in random order. A song you add while the game is running
    plays next.
  - In the game: N skips to the next song, M turns the music on or off.
  - Take every song out to get the built-in soundtrack back.

Uninstalling the game leaves the songs you put here in place.
